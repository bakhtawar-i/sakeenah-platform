# Sajaa design spec (v3)

Name: Sajaa. Arabic: سجى (never سكينة). Tagline: "Set it down here. One calm reflection a day."

Colours: ink #3A1C1E, cream #FFF7F0 (page background), maroon #88393C (primary, headings, buttons),
soft clay #7A5558 and #6B4244 (secondary text), turquoise #81D1D0, mint #B7F1E0, coral #E78861,
blush #FBE3DC. Bougainvillea accents: magenta #C9306F, pink #D860A8, red #CC2D4A, orange #F26B4F.
Never white text on coral (contrast 2.6). Ink on cream is 14.5:1.

Type: Readex Pro for all Latin text (headings 600, body 400/500). Aref Ruqaa 700 for the Arabic wordmark only.

Interface: clean and solid. Buttons are maroon, pill-shaped, cream text. Feeling pills have a plain
white fill with a thin border, and maroon with cream text when selected. No colour dots on pills.
No grain or wobble on interface elements.

Artwork: hand-drawn bougainvillea clusters, leaves and twigs at the screen edges only, never behind
text, inputs or pills. Welcome screen: top-right and bottom corners. Home: one small cluster to the
right of the heading, and clusters in the bottom corners.

Motion: on load the flowers and leaves sway slowly (about 3.6s per cycle, a few degrees), and a few
squiggly wind lines drift. Disable all of it when prefers-reduced-motion is set.

Home screen: heading "What's on your mind?", textarea, counter "300 characters remaining",
"What feels closest right now?", 16 feeling pills, Reflect button.
Welcome screen: Arabic wordmark on an arched window, "SAJAA", "Set it down here.", "One calm
reflection a day.", Begin button, privacy footer.
